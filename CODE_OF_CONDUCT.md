# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in CodeVerse a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect different opinions, experiences, and expertise.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized language or imagery, unwanted contact, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile at https://github.com/MayankJha8274. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate to protect the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and whenever an individual is representing the CodeVerse project or its community.
